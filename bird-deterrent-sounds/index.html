安全にご利用ください

カラスやネズミは音を学習し、同じ音への反応が変わる可能性があります。カラス対策にはPro版のランダム再生や重ね再生をお勧めします。梟の音でカラスが近づく場合もあり、ネズミの反応は種類や環境によって異なります。効果は保証されません。周囲に配慮し、安全な音量で使用してください。

サポート プライバシーポリシー

Bird & Rodent Sounds

Select a sound and duration, then tap Play. All nine recordings can be tried for crows; only the owl recording is presented for rodents. The guide includes eight birds and three urban rodents. Playback history stays on the device. The free version includes three 10-second plays per day, reset at local midnight. After using them, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to restore three plays. Premium unlocks continuous playback, up to nine layered sounds, a Pro-only Random button, and quick launch on iOS 18 or later. Crows and rodents can learn, so responses may change. Results are not guaranteed.
